🍗 Flappy Fwayd Chicken: Lava Run

Overview

Flappy Fried Chicken: Lava Run is a modern, browser-based arcade game built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
You control a funny, cartoon whole cooked chicken as it jumps over obstacles and avoids the flowing lava floor.

This game features:

A funny cooked chicken with a wobbling head and stubby wings

Cartoon-style sky with fluffy clouds

Animated lava floor

Score and high score tracking

Start, restart, and game over screens

Smooth physics and responsive gameplay

🕹 How to Play

Open index.html in a modern browser.

Click Start to begin the game.

Make the chicken jump by:

Clicking anywhere in the game screen, or

Pressing the Spacebar

Avoid the lava and obstacles.

Try to get the highest score possible.

When you die, click Restart to play again.

🎨 Design Notes

Chicken: Whole roasted chicken with head, eyes, beak, and legs. Wobbles for cartoon effect.

Clouds: Soft, looping clouds in the sky for visual comfort.

Lava: Animated repeating pattern, cartoon-style, not harsh on eyes.

Obstacles: Simple rectangular blocks to jump over.

⚡ Features

Mobile and desktop friendly.

High score stored locally in localStorage.

Smooth and simple arcade mechanics.

Clean, cartoonish design for eye comfort.

Easy to customize and extend.

💻 How to Customize

Change chicken colors in style.css to make different skins.

Adjust gravity, jump power, or obstacle speed in script.js for difficulty tuning.

Add new obstacle types or power-ups for extra fun.
